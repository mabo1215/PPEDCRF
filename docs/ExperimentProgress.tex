\section*{Scope of This Revision Cycle}

The active review is the third independent review recorded in
\texttt{docs/RevisionSuggestions.tex} (7 September 2026), conducted against
IEEE TIFS. It supersedes the ICME-oriented and 4 September TIFS assessments
in that file's earlier versions.

Its central conclusion is that the manuscript's \emph{negative} result
(allocation is inert) is settled and well evidenced, while its \emph{positive}
result (direction is where the leverage is) has three open gaps --- R1, R2 and
R3 below --- that were found by reading the implementation rather than the
manuscript, and that cannot be closed by editing text alone. Percentages
describe repository completion, not scientific completion; no smoke test or
proxy-only result is eligible for a real-place claim.
