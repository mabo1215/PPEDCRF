\section*{Where This Cycle Stands}

The active review is the eighth independent review in
\texttt{docs/RevisionSuggestions.tex} (10 September 2026, IEEE TIFS, verdict
\emph{minor revision}), findings R1--R7. \textbf{All seven are closed},
together with all three presentation notes. The cycle's substance was a
correction: the second dataset added last cycle had its headline positive
result reported on the query as the unit of inference, which this paper's own
protocol rejects when queries cluster. Recomputed on the unit the protocol
prescribes, that interval covers zero, and the manuscript now says so.

\paragraph{Compute state.} \textbf{Nothing is running, and no remote host was
used.} 2c (2$\times$RTX~4080 SUPER), vGPU~3090 and PRO~6000 were each checked
for the R5 data and none held it; the data was on the local G: drive, so the
whole cycle ran on the RTX~3070. The R5 arms took about 25 minutes of wall
clock between them, against a measured 7 minutes of pure compute --- the
difference is reading 1408$\times$376 PNGs off an external drive, which is why
moving the job to a faster GPU would have made it slower, not quicker.

\paragraph{Document state.} \texttt{main.pdf} \textbf{13 pages} against the
13-page TIFS ceiling; abstract \textbf{246} words (window 150--250);
\texttt{supplementary.pdf} \textbf{6 pages}, the SPS ceiling without
Editor-in-Chief approval; \texttt{titlepage.pdf} 1 page; the extended evidence
report, released with the code and not submitted, 12 pages. All four build with
\textbf{0 undefined references, 0 undefined citations and 0 overfull boxes}.
This cycle added roughly 90 lines of required material --- the second dataset
among them --- to documents already at both ceilings, and paid for all of it:
Related Work compressed, and three supplementary tables moved to the extended
report (the budget sweep, whose figure plots the same data, and the mined
proxy's per-rule and two-city E1 tables, which a real second dataset makes the
least load-bearing things in a six-page supplement).

\paragraph{Verification state.} \textbf{388 claims verify against raw exports;
0 mismatched, 0 unverifiable, 0 missing from their source file}, up from 333.
The additions this cycle are the two placement conditions that had been run but
never reported, and the place-clustered endpoints of the KITTI-360 direction
contrast --- the numbers that decide whether the claim can be made at all. \emph{A correction to an earlier statement in
this file:} the auditor does not cover every printed number. It covers every
table cell in both documents plus the registered prose claims; 74 of the 182
distinct three- and four-decimal literals in the manuscript still have no
claim, most of them interval endpoints quoted beside a covered point estimate.

\paragraph{A new check.} \texttt{check\_cross\_document\_refs.py} resolves
every literal \texttt{Table~S$n$} / \texttt{Fig.~S$n$} in the manuscript
against the supplement's actual float order, following \verb|\input| into the
generated tables. This is the class of defect R2 found: LaTeX cannot see a
reference into a separately compiled document, so the build reported zero
undefined references while the manuscript pointed at the wrong table. The
manuscript now carries no literal S-number at all, and the check fails loudly
if one is reintroduced and goes stale.

from $42\%$ to $100\%$ of the gallery frames within 25\,m. \\

allocation null is unaffected --- no placement separates from zero under either
unit. A place is also a road stretch rather than a 25\,m ball, so absolute
Top-1 is not comparable with the MSLS tables. \\

\section*{Closed in the Seventh Cycle}

R1--R4 and R6--R10 of the seventh review are closed, with the evidence in \texttt{docs/progress.md}.
Four were integrity defects found by following a reference: two manuscript
pointers that promised material the supplement did not contain (the
learning-rate sweep and the MixVPR adaptive analysis, which are in the extended
report, and the MixVPR per-placement values, which existed in an uncommitted
run and are now a supplementary table); one literal table number that had gone
stale under renumbering; and one significance marker on a pooled cell that was
never tested. The fifth, the proxy-versus-real calibration, quoted two numbers
that were in no table --- they are now scoped, printed with their manifest,
backbone and gallery, and registered as claims. Two measurements were run to
close findings rather than to add results: the strong-attacker per-placement
export, and the segmenter spread re-scored at native resolution, which shows
the six models span $0.1255$ there against $0.1216$ at working resolution but
redistribute the gaps, so the declared tolerance is generous on either scale.
