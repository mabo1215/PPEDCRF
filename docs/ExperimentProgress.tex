\section*{Closed in This Cycle}

R1--R10 are closed, with the evidence recorded in \texttt{docs/progress.md}
entries 398--443. The four items that needed real work rather than wording
were: the utility frontier's uncertainty and third seed (R5); the joint
measurement on the MSLS query frames the attack is scored on (R9, option~a);
the cross-architecture attacker (R3's optional arm); and the segmenter-spread
measurement that gives the declared tolerance a reference scale (R9's second
half). Three defects were found by the checks rather than by review: two
statistics errors caught by the joint measurement's clean-frame gate, twelve
stale frontier claims exposed once the auditor's locator check was fixed to
load every declared source, and two E1 interval endpoints that no bootstrap
reproduces, which is why those tables are now generated rather than
hand-maintained.
