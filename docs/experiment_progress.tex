\paragraph{Current execution state (1 September 2026, local session).} vGPU
3090 was found powered off (\texttt{Connection refused} on its known SSH
port) at the start of this session; rather than wait on it, E2's sigma
sweep was re-scoped to the local RTX 3070 and completed there in full, so
vGPU 3090 no longer gates E2. The public-data blockers for E1 (MSLS) and E5
(KITTI-360) are also cleared: both were registered by the author, downloaded
via direct HTTPS (working around a mid-session WSL-level DNS resolver outage
with a Cloudflare-DoH-resolved \texttt{curl --resolve} fallback, then via
PowerShell once confirmed healthy), extracted locally, and manifest-built.
E1's manifest build initially silently dropped one of the two requested
cities (a real bug in the eligible-query selection, fixed); after the fix
and two more real bug fixes in the benchmark runner itself (shared-gallery
handling, missing raw baseline), E1 produced complete all, o2n, and n2o
place-labeled runs and they are now integrated into both paper files. The
o2n/n2o runs are official cross-time splits, but their realized subset has
limited viewpoint/illumination/season/weather diversity. The original E5
checkpoint's diagnostic gate failed on the real KITTI-360 manifest, confirming
the checkpoint-provenance problem. A replacement mask-backed checkpoint now
passes the operational non-constant gate on held-out sequence 0002, but its
near-zero attribution agreement is negative evidence rather than validation.
E3/E4/E6/E7 required no vGPU time and were completed on
the local RTX 3070 / CPU from already-existing per-query exports.

\paragraph{Evidence policy.} The existing 12/50-pair benchmark remains a
visual-similarity proxy. E1 is now the paper's one true-place/geolocation
result (gate-passing, \texttt{scientific\_evidence=true}), reported alongside
the proxy with an explicit note that its effect size is much smaller, its
cross-time direction is condition-dependent, and its condition-diversity
coverage is limited --- it strengthens, but does not replace, the proxy
claims. E2--E7 strengthen mechanism, utility, and attacker-sensitivity
claims, but incomplete, smoke, blocked, or
protocol-mismatched outputs
remain engineering evidence and must not be inserted into the manuscript.
Every number integrated into \texttt{paper/main.tex} or
\texttt{paper/appendix.tex} in this session was read directly from a
non-smoke run's CSV/JSON export (\texttt{scientific\_evidence} audited
manually where the export script's own flag defaults to false pending
review) and cross-checked against the numbers already cited elsewhere in the
manuscript for consistency.

\paragraph{Additional local/vGPU verification (1 September 2026).} The vGPU
3090 was subsequently powered on and re-checked through the credentials in the
local environment file: the pinned host key matched, the RTX 3090 exposed
49152 MiB, and the remote PyTorch environment reported CUDA availability.
The earlier six-attacker proxy12/proxy50 checks completed with 5,436 and
22,650 per-query rows, respectively. To close the previously blocked CLIP
verification, the two public CLIP checkpoints were then downloaded through
\texttt{https://hf-mirror.com}, loaded successfully in strict offline mode,
and used in complete eight-attacker runs. The mirror-backed proxy12 run
contains 7,236 per-query rows and 219 summary rows; the proxy50 run contains
30,150 per-query rows and 219 summary rows. Both include CLIP ViT-B/32 and
ViT-L/14, use CUDA, pass finite-value audits, and use the checkpoint whose
SHA-256 matches the local copy. These runs remain proxy engineering evidence
(\texttt{scientific\_evidence=false}) and do not replace the paper's existing
validated export or alter its headline claims. Separately, the optional Manila
s2w MSLS run completed locally with 80 queries, 80 gallery images, and three
seeds; its raw/full Top-1 values were 0.5375 and $0.5000\pm0.0177$,
respectively. Because this run is single-city and small-gallery evidence, it
is not written into the paper's headline claims.
