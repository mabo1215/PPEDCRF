E2 & Matched PSNR/MSE/energy and unary-only/no-DCRF ablations (R2-2, R3-3) & \done & Pivoted off vGPU 3090 entirely: all local data/weights/checkpoint were already present, so the full 12-point sigma sweep (\{4,6,8,10,12,16,20,24,28,32,40,50\}, ResNet18, proxy12 scale, real monitoring corpus) ran sequentially on the local RTX 3070 in about 20 minutes (each point under 2 minutes). \texttt{matched\_psnr\_from\_sweep.py} produced the matched-PSNR/effective-MSE table at 30/33/36\,dB targets; a real schema-mismatch bug in that script (it read a non-existent \texttt{label} column from \texttt{summary.csv}, which only carries the numeric aggregate) was found and fixed. Headline finding: at matched PSNR the six sigma-tunable variants (full/no\_temporal/no\_ncp/unary\_only/no\_dcrf/global\_noise) are statistically indistinguishable in Top-1 (0.639 at 30\,dB, 0.667 at 33\,dB, 0.722 at 36\,dB across all six) --- PPEDCRF's DCRF/NCP/temporal mechanism buys better quality at matched privacy, not more privacy at matched quality. The deterministic mask-guided blur/mosaic baselines have no PSNR-tunable knob and could not be matched to the swept targets (their fixed native point sits 2--10\,dB below the targets), so their lower Top-1 at that lower quality is reported with an explicit PSNR-gap column rather than claimed as a matched-quality comparison. (An earlier verbal report of this result had the blur/mosaic direction backwards; corrected before writeback.) Integrated into \texttt{paper/appendix.tex} (new \S~Matched-PSNR/Effective-MSE Comparison) and five corresponding passages in \texttt{paper/main.tex}; both PDFs rebuild with 0 errors. \\

\paragraph{Current execution state (1 September 2026, local session).} vGPU
3090 was found powered off (\texttt{Connection refused} on its known SSH
port) at the start of this session; rather than wait on it, E2's sigma
sweep was re-scoped to the local RTX 3070 and completed there in full, so
vGPU 3090 no longer gates E2. The public-data blockers for E1 (MSLS) and E5
(KITTI-360) are also cleared: both were registered by the author, downloaded
via direct HTTPS (working around a mid-session WSL-level DNS resolver outage
with a Cloudflare-DoH-resolved \texttt{curl --resolve} fallback, then via
PowerShell once confirmed healthy), extracted locally, and manifest-built.
E1's manifest build initially silently dropped one of the two requested
cities (a real bug in the eligible-query selection, fixed); after the fix
and two more real bug fixes in the benchmark runner itself (shared-gallery
handling, missing raw baseline), E1 produced its first real place-labeled
result and it is now integrated into both paper files. E5's diagnostic gate
still fails --- now demonstrated on the real KITTI-360 manifest rather than
the monitoring proxy --- confirming the checkpoint-provenance blocker is
data-independent. E3/E4/E6/E7 required no vGPU time and were completed on
the local RTX 3070 / CPU from already-existing per-query exports.

\paragraph{Evidence policy.} The existing 12/50-pair benchmark remains a
visual-similarity proxy. E1 is now the paper's one true-place/geolocation
result (gate-passing, \texttt{scientific\_evidence=true}), reported alongside
the proxy with an explicit note that its effect size is much smaller and its
condition-diversity coverage is limited --- it strengthens, but does not
replace, the proxy claims. E2--E7 strengthen mechanism, utility, and
attacker-sensitivity claims, but incomplete, smoke, blocked, or
protocol-mismatched outputs
remain engineering evidence and must not be inserted into the manuscript.
Every number integrated into \texttt{paper/main.tex} or
\texttt{paper/appendix.tex} in this session was read directly from a
non-smoke run's CSV/JSON export (\texttt{scientific\_evidence} audited
manually where the export script's own flag defaults to false pending
review) and cross-checked against the numbers already cited elsewhere in the
manuscript for consistency.
